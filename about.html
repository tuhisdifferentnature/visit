Our story

From a home kitchen
to a quiet dream.

Tuhi's Different Nature began with Jaynab Akter — a homemaker, a mother, and a woman who believed that a small beginning can still hold a beautiful future.

For years, Jaynab's days were shaped by her family — the rhythm of a household, the care of a home. But quietly, in the spaces between everyday duties, another dream was growing. A dream of building something of her own. Something gentle. Something honest.

That dream became Tuhi's Different Nature — a lifestyle brand for women who, like her, value softness, simplicity, and things made with intention.

"I wanted to show that a homemaker is not the end of a story —
she is often the beginning of one."

— Jaynab Akter, Founder

Every piece in our collection is chosen with the same care Jaynab gives to her own home — thoughtfully, slowly, and with the women who wear it in mind. We believe in quiet quality over loud trends, and in building a brand that feels like a warm conversation rather than a crowded shop.

This is still a small journey. But it is ours — and now, it is yours too. Thank you for being part of it.
